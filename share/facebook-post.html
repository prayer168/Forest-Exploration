🌳🐻 Walk into a forest with no edge — pick any direction, you'll never hit a wall. I built "Forest Adventure," a first-person 3D web game. No quests, no score, no boss fight — just a living forest to wander around in. 🍃 ✨ What you'll run into: 🌲 Push through dense thickets into sunlit clearings, with layers of canopy overhead 🐗 Meet wild boar and Formosan muntjac — they freeze and stare, then bolt if you press closer 🐻 Spot a Formosan black bear. It isn't afraid of you, but it quietly keeps its distance 🦅 Eagles circling high above; butterflies, dragonflies and bees drifting right past your face 💧 Wade across streams with actual fish in them — and if you wander far enough, into a reed-choked swamp 🕷️ Look down and you'll find orb webs, grasshoppers, lizards, frogs, snails and rhino beetles 🎮 Dead simple: arrow keys to walk, drag the mouse to look around, scroll wheel to glide forward 😌 Lazy mode: park your cursor near the edge of the screen and the view pans itself The whole forest is grown on the fly — terrain, trees, streams and swamps are all procedurally generated. The world is endless, yet walk back the way you came and that same tree is still standing exactly where you left it. 🌱 Great for teaching ecosystems and animal behaviour — or for decompressing after a very long day. No install, no download, no bug spray required! 🦟❌ 👉 Step inside: https://prayer168.github.io/Forest-Exploration/ Know someone who keeps saying they need to get out into nature?
